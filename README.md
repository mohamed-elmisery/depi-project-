# 🛒 Sales & Customer Behavior Analysis Dashboard – Power BI

## 📌 Overview
This project is part of my graduation project in the **DEPI Power BI Specialist Track**.  
The goal is to analyze **sales performance, profitability, shipping efficiency, and customer behavior** using Power BI dashboards.  

The dataset contains:
- **Orders Data**: Sales, Profit, Discounts, Quantity, Delivery status.  
- **Customer Data**: Customer demographics, segments, location.  
- **Product Data**: Categories, departments, product details.  
- **Shipping Data**: Shipping time, late delivery risk, shipping mode.  
- **Web Visits Data**: Product views, department visits, browsing behavior.  

---

## 🎯 Project Objectives
- Build a **cleaned data model** (Star Schema) from raw flat data.  
- Create **KPIs & Measures** in DAX:
  - Total Sales, Profit, Orders, Profit Margin.  
  - Late Delivery %.  
  - Customer Segment Analysis.  
  - Product Views vs Sales (conversion analysis).  
- Develop interactive dashboards for:
  1. **Executive Overview**  
  2. **Sales & Profit Analysis**  
  3. **Shipping Performance**  
  4. **Customer & Product Insights**  
  5. **Web Visits vs Orders Funnel**  

---

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies
- **Power BI Desktop** for modeling & visualization.  
- **DAX** for measures and KPIs.  
- **Power Query** for data cleaning and transformation.  
- **GitHub** for project documentation and sharing.  

---

## 📊 Dashboards
1. **Overview Dashboard** – High-level KPIs (Sales, Profit, Orders, Views).  
2. **Sales Dashboard** – Sales trends by category, region, and time.  
3. **Profitability Dashboard** – Top/Bottom products and categories.  
4. **Shipping Dashboard** – On-time vs late deliveries, shipping modes.  
5. **Customer Behavior Dashboard** – Product views vs sales conversion.  

---

## 🚀 Key Insights
- Identified **top customers and products** driving sales.  
- Highlighted **loss-making products** despite high sales volume.  
- Found a significant % of **late deliveries** in specific regions.  
- Mapped **customer browsing behavior** to actual purchases.
